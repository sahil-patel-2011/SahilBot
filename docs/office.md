# Office documents

`create_document` makes .pdf, .docx (from Markdown), .pptx (from slides) and .xlsx (from sheets). `file_read` reads all of them back.

## Quality bar
- Lead with a short summary. Use real headings, short paragraphs, bullet lists for steps, tables for comparisons.
- Numbers with units and dates; totals checked a second way.
- Slides: one point per slide, a headline that states it, at most 5 bullets; details go in notes.
- Always re-open the file after creating it to check every section rendered.
