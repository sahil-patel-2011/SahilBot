1. Identify datums (A, B, C) and how the part is located and inspected.
2. For each feature control frame, explain in plain words what is controlled (form, orientation, location, runout, profile) and the tolerance zone.
3. When creating: tolerance only what matters functionally; position for holes (MMC where assembly allows); profile for complex surfaces.
4. Check every dimension needed to make the part appears exactly once.
