name: design-for-manufacturing
description: Make a part cheaper and easier to make (CNC, injection molding, sheet metal, 3D printing).
source: you
uses: 0
category: Mechanical Engineering
---

1. Identify the process and quantity.
2. CNC: avoid deep narrow pockets, inside corner radii at least the tool radius, standard hole sizes and threads, fewer setups.
3. Injection molding: uniform walls, 1-2 degrees of draft, ribs 50-60% of wall thickness, avoid undercuts, sensible gate location.
4. Sheet metal: bend radius at least the thickness, holes at least 2x thickness from bends, bend reliefs, consistent bend directions.
5. 3D printing (FDM): orient for strength and few supports, 45 degree overhang rule, about 0.2 mm clearance for fits, at least 2 perimeters.
6. Report each change, why, and the expected savings.
