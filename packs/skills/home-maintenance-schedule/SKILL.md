## Gather
Home type and age, climate, systems (furnace/heat pump/boiler, AC, water heater type, well/septic, sump pump, pool), filter sizes, last service dates. Save these facts to memory with the user's OK.

## Schedule (typical; follow manufacturer manuals where they differ)
- Monthly: test smoke and CO alarms; check HVAC filter (replace every 1-3 months, more with pets); run water in unused drains; check for leaks under sinks; clean range hood filter as needed.
- Spring: AC service before cooling season; clean gutters and check downspouts drain away from foundation; inspect roof (from the ground or with binoculars) and siding; test sump pump; check outdoor faucets and irrigation; screens.
- Summer: clean dryer vent duct (at least yearly; lint buildup is a fire risk); check deck and fences; trim plants away from the house and AC unit.
- Fall: furnace/heat pump service; gutters again after leaves fall; shut off and drain exterior faucets in freezing climates; seal gaps and check weatherstripping; chimney inspection and sweep if used (NFPA recommends yearly inspection).
- Winter: watch for ice dams; keep vents clear of snow; know where the main water shutoff is.
- Yearly: flush tank water heater (per manual; check anode rod every few years); test GFCI outlets; clean refrigerator coils; check caulk in tubs and showers; check fire extinguishers; replace smoke alarm batteries if not sealed (replace alarms about every 10 years, CO alarms per manufacturer, often 5-7 years).
- Septic: inspect about every 1-3 years and pump typically every 3-5 years (EPA guidance).

## Deliverable
A maintenance calendar spreadsheet (task, frequency, month, last done, next due, notes, cost) and recurring reminders via schedule or mac_apps Reminders. A home log of service visits and receipts.

## Safety
Ladders: three points of contact, firm footing, avoid power lines; roof work is best left to pros. Turn off power before servicing appliances. Gas smell: leave the house and call the gas utility or 911 from outside. See home-repairs for when to call a pro.
