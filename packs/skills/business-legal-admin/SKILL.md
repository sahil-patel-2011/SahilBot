## Ground rules
This is general information, not legal advice, and rules differ by country and state. For decisions with real money or risk, tell the user to consult a licensed lawyer (and an accountant for tax effects). Cite official sources (IRS, SBA, USPTO, state secretary of state sites, the national equivalents elsewhere) with dates.

## Entity types (US, high level)
- Sole proprietorship / general partnership: simplest, no liability shield.
- LLC: liability protection if formalities are kept; flexible taxation (default pass-through; can elect S corp treatment).
- S corporation (tax election): may reduce self-employment tax when the owner pays a reasonable salary; ownership limits.
- C corporation: preferred by venture investors (often Delaware); double taxation of dividends; possible QSBS benefits.
Choose by liability, taxes, investors, number of owners, admin cost. Admin steps: register, EIN, registered agent, operating agreement/bylaws, business bank account (never mix funds), local licenses and permits, sales tax registration if selling taxable goods, annual reports.

## Contract checklist (read every contract for)
Parties and scope; deliverables and acceptance; price, payment terms, late fees; term, renewal (auto-renew?), termination rights; IP ownership and licenses; confidentiality; warranties and disclaimers; limitation of liability and caps; indemnities; insurance; data protection (and a BAA if health data, see hipaa-* skills); non-solicit/non-compete (restricted in many places); governing law, venue, dispute resolution; assignment; signatures with authority. Flag one-sided terms and missing items; summarize in a table with risk level. For docusign, prepare but never sign or send without the user's approval.

## IP basics
Trademark: protects brand names/logos; search USPTO (or national office) and the web before choosing a name. Copyright: automatic for original works; register for stronger enforcement. Patent: inventions; public disclosure starts clocks (e.g. a 1-year US grace period; most countries have none), so talk to a patent attorney before disclosing. Trade secrets: protect with NDAs and access controls. Get IP assignment agreements from founders, employees and contractors.

## Privacy and website terms
Privacy policy must match what you actually collect and share; laws include GDPR (EU/UK), state privacy laws (e.g. California CCPA/CPRA), COPPA for children, HIPAA for covered health entities. Terms of service: acceptable use, payments, refunds, liability limits. Cookie consent where required.

## Pitfalls
Copying another company's terms; misclassifying workers as contractors; operating without required licenses; personal guarantees missed in leases and loans. When in doubt: consult a lawyer.
