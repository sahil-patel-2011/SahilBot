## Disclaimer
High-level educational checklist based on the HIPAA Privacy, Security and Breach Notification Rules; not legal advice. HHS proposed significant Security Rule updates (published January 2025; check current status before relying on "addressable" vs "required"). Consult a healthcare compliance professional or attorney; HHS/ONC's free Security Risk Assessment (SRA) Tool helps small practices.

## Governance
- [ ] Named Privacy Officer and Security Officer (can be one person in a small practice).
- [ ] Written policies and procedures; reviewed yearly; kept 6 years.
- [ ] Notice of Privacy Practices posted and given to patients; acknowledgment collected.
- [ ] Inventory of where PHI lives: EHR, billing, email, phones, laptops, paper, backups, cloud apps, AI tools.

## Administrative safeguards
- [ ] Security risk analysis (documented), updated at least yearly and after big changes; risk management plan with owners and dates.
- [ ] Workforce training at hire and yearly (phishing, minimum necessary, social media, device use); sanctions policy.
- [ ] Access management: role-based access, onboarding/offboarding checklist (revoke access same day).
- [ ] BAAs signed with every vendor handling PHI (EHR, billing, IT, cloud storage, email, answering service, shredding, AI/transcription tools); keep a BAA register with renewal dates.
- [ ] Contingency plan: data backup, disaster recovery, emergency mode operations; test restores.
- [ ] Incident response and breach procedures (hipaa-breach-response).
- [ ] Periodic review of system activity logs.

## Physical safeguards
- [ ] Facility access controls; visitor policy; locked records and server/network closets.
- [ ] Workstation placement (screens away from public), automatic screen lock, clean-desk rule.
- [ ] Device and media controls: inventory, encryption, secure disposal (shredding, certified wiping).

## Technical safeguards
- [ ] Unique user IDs; strong passwords and multi-factor authentication, especially for email, EHR and remote access.
- [ ] Encryption of laptops, phones and backups (at rest) and email/portals in transit.
- [ ] Automatic logoff; audit logs enabled and retained; integrity controls.
- [ ] Patching, endpoint protection, firewall, secure Wi-Fi (separate guest network), email filtering.
- [ ] Secure messaging or portal for patient communications.

## Patient rights processes
- [ ] Right of access requests handled within 30 days (reasonable cost-based fees only); amendment, accounting of disclosures, restrictions, confidential communications.
- [ ] Authorization forms for uses that need them (e.g. marketing).

## Pitfalls
No documented risk analysis (a frequent finding in OCR enforcement), missing BAAs, shared logins, unencrypted laptops, texting PHI on personal phones, using consumer AI tools with patient data without a BAA.
