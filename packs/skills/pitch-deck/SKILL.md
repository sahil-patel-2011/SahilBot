1. Story in 10-12 slides: problem, solution, why now, market, product, traction, business model, competition, go-to-market, team, financials, the ask.
2. One message per slide, a headline that states it, numbers with sources.
3. Create with create_document (pptx); the talk track goes in speaker notes.
4. Re-read every slide and cut words.
