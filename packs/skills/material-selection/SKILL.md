1. Requirements: loads, stiffness, temperature range, corrosion/chemicals, weight, cost, quantity, process (machined, cast, molded, printed, sheet).
2. Shortlist with real property data (yield, density, E, max service temperature) from reliable sources such as MatWeb or supplier datasheets; cite them.
3. Compare in a table, including strength-to-weight and cost per part.
4. Recommend one plus an alternative, with finishing (anodize, powder coat, heat treat) and any design changes the process needs.
