1. Define the market and the questions to answer.
2. Send helpers in parallel (agents) for market size, top competitors (pricing, positioning, reviews), customer pain points (reviews, forums) and trends.
3. Every number needs a source and a date; prefer primary and recent sources.
4. Synthesize: a competitor table, key insights, opportunities and threats, next steps.
