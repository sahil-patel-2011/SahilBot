description: Turn a meeting transcript or notes into a clean summary with action items.
source: you
uses: 0
category: Business
---

1. Read the whole transcript.
2. Write: purpose, decisions, discussion highlights, open questions, and actions (owner, task, due date). Quote numbers and commitments exactly; mark anything unclear.
3. For a document, use office meeting_notes (skill office-meetings): it formats the notes, resolves "Friday" into a date from the meeting date, and lists items you may have missed.
4. Offer to add action items as reminders or calendar events, or to draft the follow-up email.
