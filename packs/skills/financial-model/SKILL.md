1. List drivers: customers, price, conversion, churn, unit costs, salaries, rent, marketing.
2. Monthly for year 1, then yearly: revenue, COGS, gross margin, operating costs, EBITDA, cash.
3. One assumptions sheet that every other cell references; base, best and worst scenarios.
4. Compute break-even and runway; check totals a second way.
5. Save as xlsx and summarize the key numbers in plain words. It is planning, not financial advice.
