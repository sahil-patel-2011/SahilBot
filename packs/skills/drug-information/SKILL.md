1. Use the research tool first where it fits: research(source="pubmed") then "pubmed_abstract" for papers, "clinical_trials" for trials, "drug_label" for official FDA labels, "arxiv" for preprints, "doi" for citations. Then:
   Find the official label (DailyMed/FDA, EMA or the national regulator) and a reliable reference such as MedlinePlus.
2. Report indications, usual adult dose ranges as written, boxed warnings, common and serious side effects, and major interactions, with citations.
3. For interactions, check both drugs' labels and say clearly when one is significant.
4. Never recommend starting, stopping or changing a dose: that's the prescriber's decision. Suggest checking with a pharmacist.
