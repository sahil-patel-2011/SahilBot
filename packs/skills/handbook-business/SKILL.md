## Frame the decision
Identify the customer, problem, decision owner, constraints, timeframe and what would change the decision. Separate facts, assumptions, forecasts and objectives. A request for ideas can start with options; a financial or operational recommendation needs source data and stated assumptions. Use the existing project context before asking the user for details already provided.

## Choose focused expertise
Use business-plan-writing for a business plan, business-unit-economics for contribution economics, financial-model for forecasts, pricing-strategy for pricing, business-go-to-market-sales for distribution, business-customer-success for retention, business-operations-management for processes and business-decision-making for alternatives. business-fundraising supports a funding narrative with evidence; business-bookkeeping-taxes and business-legal-admin require current jurisdiction-specific sources and professional review where appropriate.

## Evidence and numbers
Keep market size, customer demand, conversion, churn, pricing and cost claims connected to dated sources or the user's real data. Do not invent interview findings, sales traction, customer quotes, competitors or citations. Compute arithmetic in a reproducible sheet/script. Separate revenue, cash collection, expenses, capital costs and financing. State the unit and period of every metric. Show base/downside/upside scenarios and the assumptions that drive the range, rather than presenting one forecast as certainty.

## Design a measurable next step
For each uncertain assumption, propose a small reversible experiment: hypothesis, audience, method, success threshold, cost/time bound and decision after the result. Define leading and lagging indicators. An operating process needs an owner, trigger, inputs, exceptions, handoff, completion check and review date. A dashboard is useful only when its measurements support an actual decision.

## Execute with clear boundaries
Draft communications and documents in the user's voice and brand. Use connected service data only within the authorized account and task. Before sending, publishing, buying, changing access or committing funds, apply the app's approval requirements. A saved preference or knowledge note does not grant authorization. Verify external actions from actual service receipts and keep uncertain outcomes unresolved until checked.

## Preserve useful knowledge
Store project requirements and decisions with dependencies so changed assumptions invalidate the affected plan. Save a reusable procedure only after a real verified result, with environment/version and limitations. Keep customer credentials and API keys out of business notes. A final brief should state the decision, evidence, major uncertainty, next owner/action and the artifact location.

Reference entry: [U.S. SBA business planning](https://www.sba.gov/counseling/plan-your-business/). Check current local legal, tax, funding and market sources for the actual decision.
