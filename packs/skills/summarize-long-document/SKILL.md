---
name: summarize-long-document
description: Summarize a long PDF, Word file or web page accurately.
source: you
uses: 0
category: Office
---

1. Read the whole thing in chunks (file_read / fetch_page with chunk numbers, read_result for long results). Don't summarize from the first page only.
2. Note the purpose, main claims, key numbers and dates, and conclusions, with page or section references.
3. Write: a 2-3 sentence summary, then bullets for the key points, then anything surprising or that needs action.
4. Quote exact figures; never round or reinterpret without saying so.
