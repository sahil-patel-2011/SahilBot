## 1. Find the meeting
Calendar (find_tools("calendar") or the Calendar app): "my 2pm" means today at 2 pm in the user's time zone; "next call with Dana" means the next event with that attendee. Get title, time, attendees (names, emails, companies), location or video link, the description and attached docs. If two events match, ask which.

## 2. Look people up (brain first)
- `about` lookup for each attendee and their company: role, relationship, preferences, past facts with dates.
- `vault` search for the client, project or deal notes; memory search for past conversations and decisions.
- Connected apps next: CRM (HubSpot, Salesforce, Pipedrive via find_tools) for contact owner, deal stage, amount, close date, last activity; LinkedIn or the company site via web search for public role and news (only what's public).

## 3. What happened last time
- Last call notes or transcript: Zoom / Google Meet / Otter / Fireflies transcripts when connected, the vault, or meeting-notes output. Pull decisions, open questions and action items.
- Recent emails (last ~30 days, mail or Gmail search by attendee): what they asked, what was sent, anything unanswered.
- Open promises both ways: what the user owes ("send pricing by Tuesday") and what they owe ("intro to their CFO"), each with source and date. Flag the user's overdue ones first.

## 4. Build the brief (one screen)
- Header: meeting, time, link, attendees (role · company · one line each).
- Why this meeting / goal: in one sentence (from the invite or the last thread).
- Where things stand: deal stage, amount, close date, last activity; or the project status.
- Since last time: 3-5 bullets.
- Open promises: ours / theirs, with due dates.
- Suggested agenda: 3-5 items with time boxes.
- 3 questions to ask: aimed at the open risks and the next step.
- Risks and watch-outs: stalled deal, unanswered email, new stakeholder, budget or competitor mentions, sensitive topics.
- Desired outcome: the concrete next step to leave with.
Every fact gets a source chip: "Email · Oct 14", "HubSpot · deal stage", "Zoom transcript · Nov 3", "Vault · Acme project". Unknowns say "not found", never guessed.

## 5. After
Offer: a reminder 10 minutes before (schedule), a draft of the promised follow-ups, and meeting-notes after the call to update the vault and CRM (CRM writes only with the user's OK).

## Pitfalls
Prepping the wrong event (check date and time zone); stale CRM data presented as fact; dumping every email instead of what matters; private personal details in a brief others might see. For legal or financial negotiations in the meeting, suggest the user consult a lawyer or accountant beforehand.
