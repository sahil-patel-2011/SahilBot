## Timeline (adjust to the time available)
- 8 weeks: budget; decide movers vs truck vs pods; get 3 in-home or video quotes (binding or not-to-exceed preferred); declutter (home-decluttering); start a move folder/spreadsheet.
- 6 weeks: book movers; check interstate movers' USDOT number and complaint history on FMCSA (protectyourmove.gov); order supplies; notify landlord; school transfer records; medical and vet records; plan pet and plant moves.
- 4 weeks: USPS change of address (usps.com: beware lookalike sites charging fees); schedule utilities stop/start (electric, gas, water, trash, internet); update address with employer, banks, insurance, subscriptions, voter registration, DMV (deadlines vary by state).
- 2 weeks: pack non-essentials room by room; confirm movers in writing; arrange parking/elevator; refill prescriptions.
- Moving week: pack essentials; defrost freezer; first-night box; photograph electronics' wiring; keep valuables, documents and medicines with you.
- Moving day: walk-through with movers, check inventory, photos of old place for deposit, final meter readings, hand over keys.
- After: check items against inventory and note damage before signing; file claims quickly; change locks; test smoke/CO alarms; find main water shutoff and breaker panel; register car if out of state.

## Packing system
Label each box with room + contents + number; color code by room; heavy items in small boxes; a running inventory spreadsheet (box #, room, contents, value); "open first" boxes.

## First-night box
Medicines, phone chargers, toiletries, toilet paper, towels, sheets, change of clothes, basic tools, snacks, water, pet supplies, important documents.

## Budget
Movers or truck rental, fuel, supplies, deposits, overlap rent, cleaning, tips, new furniture. Spreadsheet with estimates vs actual.

## Rules
Never book or pay movers or sign contracts yourself; draft emails for quotes and let the user send. Offer reminders for each milestone (schedule or mac_apps).

## Pitfalls
Very low quotes with big deposits (scam warning sign), not getting estimates in writing, forgetting address updates for insurance and banks, packing documents in the truck.
