## Goals: OKRs
3-5 objectives per quarter (qualitative, inspiring), each with 2-4 key results (measurable, with baseline and target, e.g. "Raise repeat purchase rate from 22% to 30%"). Owners per KR, weekly check-in, scored at quarter end (0-1.0; ~0.7 on stretch goals is fine). Tasks are not key results.

## Hiring
1. Scorecard before job post: mission of the role, 3-6 outcomes for the first year, competencies.
2. Structured interviews: same questions for every candidate, scored rubric; a work sample beats gut feel.
3. References focused on outcomes. Offer with clear comp, start date, contingencies.
4. Onboarding plan: 30/60/90 days, buddy, first win in week one.
Employment law (classification of contractors vs employees, discrimination, interview questions you can't ask, at-will, overtime) varies by place: consult an employment lawyer or HR professional.

## Managing people
Weekly 1:1s owned by the report (their agenda first), clear expectations in writing, feedback that is specific and timely (situation, behavior, impact), recognition in public, correction in private. Underperformance: clarify expectations, document, support, set a timeline; involve HR/legal before termination.

## Meetings
Every meeting has an owner, purpose, agenda and decision log; default 25 or 50 minutes; end with actions (owner + date). Replace status meetings with written updates. Use meeting-notes for summaries.

## Delegation
Delegate outcomes, not tasks. Levels: do as told → research and recommend → decide and inform → full ownership. State the level, the deadline, the check-in points and what "done" means.

## Processes and SOPs
Document what is repeated, risky or done by many people. SOP template: purpose, scope, owner, trigger, steps (numbered, one action each, with screenshots/links), quality checks, exceptions, revision date. Test it by having someone else follow it. Store in the knowledge vault or shared drive; create with create_document (.docx/.pdf).

## KPI dashboard
5-10 metrics max: leading (pipeline, activation) and lagging (revenue, churn, cash). Each with owner, target, frequency, source (Stripe, QuickBooks/Xero, HubSpot connectors). Build as a spreadsheet with charts or a page; review weekly.

## Vendor management
Define requirements, get 3 quotes, check references and security/privacy terms, negotiate (business-negotiation), track contracts, renewal dates and owners in one sheet; set reminders with schedule 60 days before renewal. Review vendors yearly.

## Pitfalls
Too many priorities, founders as bottleneck, hiring for speed over fit, undocumented knowledge, no cash visibility. Verify any legal/HR step with a professional.
