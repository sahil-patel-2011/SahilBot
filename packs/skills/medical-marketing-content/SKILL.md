1. Research the topic from medical sources and cite them; check every fact and number twice.
2. Write for patients: helpful, plain and calm, with one clear call to action (book a visit).
3. Compliance: no guaranteed outcomes, no patient-identifying details or photos without consent (HIPAA), testimonials only as allowed, FTC rules on claims, the practice's brand voice.
4. SEO: one main topic per page, a descriptive title and meta description, headings that answer real questions, local terms for the practice's area.
5. Add "for general information, not medical advice" and flag anything for the physician to review.
