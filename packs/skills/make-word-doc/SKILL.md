description: Write a well-formatted Word document (docx): letters, proposals, reports.
source: you
uses: 0
category: Office
---

1. Confirm the purpose and audience; match the tone (formal letter vs. internal memo).
2. Structure with real headings (Heading 1/2), short paragraphs, and lists; add a table where data compares.
3. Create with create_document (format docx). Put the most important point in the first paragraph.
4. Re-open with file_read and proofread: names, dates, numbers, spelling.
5. Report the path.
