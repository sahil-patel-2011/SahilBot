## Support foundations
- Channels: start with email + help center; add chat only if you can staff it. Publish hours and response targets.
- Triage: tag every ticket by type (bug, how-to, billing, feature request, complaint) and priority (P1 outage/security/billing error → minutes-hours; P2 blocked user → same day; P3 question → 1 business day). Track first response time, resolution time, reopen rate, CSAT.
- Macros: write reusable answers for the top 20 questions; personalize the first line.
- Help center: one article per question, titled as the customer asks it, with steps and screenshots; link from macros. Deflection is good only when the article truly solves the issue.
- Connectors: Intercom, Help Scout, Front, Zendesk-like tools via find_tools; pull tickets to find top drivers.

## Handling an upset customer
Acknowledge feelings and the specific problem, apologize for the experience (without admitting legal fault when it's disputed), state what you'll do and by when, do it, follow up. Offer a remedy proportional to the impact. Draft with everyday-writing-messages tone rules; never send without the user's approval.

## Customer success (recurring revenue)
- Onboarding: define "first value" (the moment customers get the result) and the steps to reach it; measure time to first value; check in at day 1, 7, 30.
- Health score: weighted mix of usage (logins, key actions), adoption breadth, support sentiment, payment status, champion still present. Red accounts get a save plan.
- Churn prevention: exit surveys and cancel-flow reasons; fix top reasons; offer pause/downgrade before cancel where fair; follow consumer laws on easy cancellation (e.g. US FTC and state click-to-cancel rules) - check current rules.
- Renewals and expansion: start 90 days before renewal; business review showing value delivered (numbers).

## Feedback loop
Monthly: top 5 ticket drivers, top feature requests with counts and revenue attached, churn reasons. Send to product owner. Add what customers say in public (reviews, Reddit, app stores) with business-complaint-mining. Close the loop with customers when things ship.

## Metrics
CSAT (% satisfied), NPS (promoters - detractors; use for trends, not as gospel), gross and net revenue retention, logo churn, time to first value, ticket volume per 100 customers.

## Pitfalls
Measuring speed only; scripts that sound robotic; ignoring silent churn (no tickets, no usage); promising roadmap dates. For refund or warranty legal obligations and data requests (GDPR/CCPA), consult a lawyer.
