## Define
Purpose and vibe, date options, guest count range, budget (total), location type, must-haves, dietary needs and accessibility. Check memory for the honoree's tastes.

## Plan backward from the date
- 8-12 weeks (bigger events 6-12 months): budget, guest list draft, venue holds, save-the-dates.
- 4-6 weeks: invitations, vendors (caterer, cake, photographer, rentals, entertainment) with 2-3 quotes each, decor plan.
- 2 weeks: RSVP chase, final headcount to vendors, shopping list, playlist, seating if needed.
- Week of: confirm vendors, prep schedule, day-of timeline, weather backup.
- Day after: thank-yous, returns, pay vendors on time.

## Budget split (rule of thumb, adjust)
Food and drink often 40-50%; venue 20-30%; decor, entertainment, favors the rest; 10% buffer. Spreadsheet with estimate vs actual.

## Food quantities (rough planning numbers; adjust for crowd and duration)
Appetizers only: ~10-12 pieces per person for a 2-3 hour party; with a meal: ~4-6 pieces. Main protein ~1/3-1/2 lb (150-225 g) cooked per adult. Drinks ~1 per person per hour plus water. Ice ~1 lb per guest. Keep food safety in mind: hot foods hot, cold foods cold, the 2-hour rule (see home-recipes-cooking).

## Deliverables
Planning checklist with dates and owners, budget sheet, guest list/RSVP tracker (spreadsheet), vendor comparison, run-of-show (time, what happens, who's responsible), shopping list, invitation wording drafts.

## Rules
Never send invitations, emails or messages, sign vendor contracts or pay deposits: draft them and let the user approve and act. Read vendor contracts for cancellation and deposit terms (business-legal-admin checklist ideas apply).

## Pitfalls
Inviting before confirming the venue, under-ordering ice and chairs, no rain plan, forgetting kids' or dietary needs, no point person on the day.
