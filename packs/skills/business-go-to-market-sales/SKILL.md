## GTM plan in six decisions
1. ICP: industry, size, role of buyer and user, trigger events (new funding, hiring, regulation), disqualifiers.
2. Value proposition per segment: pain, outcome, proof.
3. Motion matched to price: under ~$1k/yr self-serve and product-led; ~$5k-$50k inside sales; large contracts field/enterprise sales with longer cycles. Treat as rough guidance.
4. Channels: outbound, inbound content/SEO, partners/resellers, marketplaces, events, referrals. Pick 1-2 to start.
5. Pricing and packaging (pricing-strategy).
6. Metrics: pipeline created, win rate, cycle length, average deal size, CAC payback.

## Pipeline stages (with exit criteria, not feelings)
Lead → Qualified (fits ICP + has pain; use BANT or MEDDICC-lite: budget, authority, need, timing, decision process, champion) → Discovery done → Demo/proposal → Negotiation → Closed won/lost (record reason). Keep it in HubSpot/Pipedrive/Close via find_tools, or a spreadsheet.

## Prospecting
Build a list of 50-100 ICP accounts with web research (and Apollo/Crunchbase connectors when connected). Personalize the first line to a real trigger; one clear ask (15-minute call). Sequence: 3-5 touches over ~2-3 weeks across email/LinkedIn/phone. Follow anti-spam rules (CAN-SPAM, GDPR/PECR where relevant): honest subject, opt-out, real address. Draft with the email skills; never send without the user's approval.

## Discovery call (talk ~30%, listen ~70%)
Current situation, pain and its cost, what they've tried, impact if unsolved, decision process and timeline, budget range, next step with a date. Summarize back what you heard. Before each call, build a brief with business-meeting-prep (brain first: `about` for the account and contacts, then CRM and email).

## Objections
Acknowledge, clarify ("When you say too expensive, compared to what?"), respond with proof, confirm. Price objections are often value objections: return to the quantified cost of the problem.

## Close and forecast
Mutual action plan with dates; proposal with options; ask for the decision. Forecast = sum of deal value x stage probability, adjusted for deal age; review stuck deals weekly.

## Deliverables
GTM one-pager, sales playbook doc (ICP, scripts, objection library, stage definitions), prospect list spreadsheet, outreach drafts. For contracts and terms, consult a lawyer; see business-legal-admin.

## Verify
Every prospect fact from a source you read; pipeline math reconciles with CRM exports; no outreach sent without the user's yes.
