## Positioning first (April Dunford-style questions, in your own words)
Competitive alternatives (what customers would do otherwise) → unique attributes → the value those enable → who cares most (best-fit customers) → market category that makes the value obvious. Output a positioning statement and 3 key messages, each with a proof point (data, testimonial, demo).

## Channel selection
Match channel to where buyers already look and to unit economics:
- Intent capture: search ads, SEO, marketplaces, review sites. Best when people already search for the solution.
- Demand creation: social, content, video, podcasts, events, PR, partnerships.
- Owned: email list, community, referral programs.
Start with 1-2 channels, run them well for 60-90 days, measure CAC by channel before adding more.

## Content
Pick topics from customer questions (sales calls, support tickets, forums, People Also Ask). Each piece has one audience, one job (attract, educate, convert) and one call to action. Repurpose one deep piece into 5-10 small ones. For keyword research, audits and briefs use the seo-research and seo-audit skills.

## Paid ads basics
- Define conversion and tracking first (pixel/conversion API, UTM tags, offline conversion import for leads).
- Small structured tests: 2-3 audiences x 2-3 creatives, a fixed budget per cell, run until each cell has enough conversions to compare (aim for dozens, not single digits).
- Judge by cost per acquisition and downstream revenue, not clicks. Kill losers, scale winners ~20-30% at a time.
- Health and finance ads have extra platform rules; check the current policy pages.

## Email
Welcome series, nurture by segment, newsletters with real value; clean lists; follow CAN-SPAM/GDPR (consent, opt-out, address). Klaviyo/Brevo/HubSpot connectors when available.

## Budget and measurement
Budget = target new customers x allowable CAC (from business-unit-economics). Dashboard: spend, leads, CAC, conversion by channel, revenue attributed (state the attribution model; last-click undercounts awareness channels).

## Deliverables
Positioning doc, 90-day plan, content calendar spreadsheet, ad test plan, draft copy. Never publish posts or launch ads without the user's approval.

## Pitfalls and caveats
Marketing to "everyone", copying competitors' messaging, judging on vanity metrics, unsupported claims (FTC truth-in-advertising; healthcare claims need extra care, see medical-marketing-content). For trademark or advertising-law questions consult a lawyer.
