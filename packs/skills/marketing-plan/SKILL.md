1. Goal and audience (who, what they need, where they spend time).
2. Positioning and three key messages with proof points.
3. Channels with budget and expected results (SEO, paid search, social, email, referrals, partnerships), using benchmarks you can cite.
4. A 90-day calendar and the metrics to track (cost per acquisition, conversion, lifetime value).
5. Deliver as a document plus a budget spreadsheet.
