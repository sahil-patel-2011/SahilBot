description: Set or review pricing for a product or service.
source: you
uses: 0
category: Business
---

1. Gather unit and fixed costs, competitor prices (with sources) and the value to the customer.
2. Consider cost-plus, value-based, tiered (good/better/best), subscription and usage-based models.
3. Model margins and break-even at each price in a spreadsheet.
4. Recommend prices and packaging with the reasoning and how to test them.
