1. Use the research tool first where it fits: research(source="pubmed") then "pubmed_abstract" for papers, "clinical_trials" for trials, "drug_label" for official FDA labels, "arxiv" for preprints, "doi" for citations. Then:
   Read the abstract and the full text when available.
2. Extract the question, design, population and size, intervention and comparator, primary outcome with effect size and confidence interval, adverse events and follow-up.
3. Judge limitations (bias, size, funding, generalizability) and how it fits with guidelines.
4. Write a plain-language summary plus a structured table, citing the paper with DOI or PMID.
