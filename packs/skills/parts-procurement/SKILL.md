1. Build a parts list from the user's requirements. Record manufacturer part numbers, quantities, package or mechanical interface, and any acceptable substitutes.
2. Confirm fit against the manufacturer datasheet or drawing. For electrical parts check voltage, current, temperature, tolerance, package, and pinout. For mechanical parts check dimensions, thread, finish, and load limits.
3. Compare reputable suppliers. Record live stock, minimum order, pack size, unit and extended cost, lead time, and delivered total when available. Separate supplier SKU from manufacturer part number.
4. Put explicitly requested items into the cart when authorized. Verify quantities and the exact variants after adding them. Capture shipping estimates and flag any substitution or unavailable item.
5. Give the user the verified cart and total. Use the purchase gate for checkout, including payment, final shipping, and any changes to the quoted total. Report an order as placed only after a receipt or confirmation appears.
