1. One goal per email; a subject under 50 characters that says what's inside.
2. Open with why it matters to them, then the ask, then one clear next step.
3. Short (under 150 words), plain and personal; no hype.
4. Sequences: 3-4 touches, 3-5 days apart, each adding something new.
5. Draft for the user to review; sending needs their OK.
