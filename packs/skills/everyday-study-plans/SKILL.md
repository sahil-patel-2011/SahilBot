## Evidence-based techniques (from cognitive psychology research)
- Retrieval practice: testing yourself beats re-reading and highlighting. Use practice questions, blank-page recall, flashcards.
- Spaced repetition: review at increasing intervals (e.g. 1 day, 3 days, 1 week, 2 weeks); apps like Anki automate it.
- Interleaving: mix problem types in practice sessions once basics are learned.
- Elaboration and self-explanation: explain why and how, connect to examples; the Feynman technique (teach it simply, find gaps).
- Worked examples, then fading to solo problems.
- Sleep consolidates memory; cramming all night backfires.

## Exam plan (work backward)
1. Inputs: exam date, topics/syllabus and weights, current level per topic (quick diagnostic), hours available per day, other commitments.
2. Allocate time by weight x weakness. Reserve the last 20-25% of time for full practice exams under timed conditions and review of errors.
3. Daily blocks: 25-50 minutes focused + 5-10 minute breaks (Pomodoro-style); hardest topics at the best energy time; mix review of old topics daily.
4. Error log: every missed question → why (knowledge gap, misread, careless) → fix.
5. Build the plan as a spreadsheet calendar and set reminders via schedule or mac_apps.

## Learning a new skill
Define a concrete goal and project ("hold a 10-minute conversation in Spanish by June"), break into sub-skills, practice the hardest sub-skill deliberately with fast feedback, track hours and progress weekly, adjust.

## Materials and tools
Official syllabus and past papers first; reputable courses; the user's notes via knowledge_search; generate practice questions and flashcards from their material (check facts against the source); summaries with citations. Save the plan and progress to memory with their OK.

## Focus and motivation
Phone in another room, website blockers, start with a 5-minute task to beat procrastination, study buddy, reward after sessions. Persistent focus problems, anxiety or burnout: suggest talking to a school counselor or clinician (see health-mental-health-support).

## Pitfalls
Planning without a diagnostic, only passive review, no practice exams, ignoring sleep.
