1. Establish the area, dining date, per-person budget, and firm constraints. Use known preferences and make reversible assumptions about the rest.
2. Check the restaurant's own menu, hours, and booking page. Check accessibility and dietary requirements directly when they determine suitability. Mark details that need confirmation.
3. Read several recent reviews across available sources. Report recurring themes, the review period, and disagreements. Keep specific reported experiences distinct from restaurant-confirmed facts.
4. Filter for hard requirements before ranking. Compare likely meal price, transit, noise, booking availability, and a realistic backup. Link the sources used.
5. Prepare a reservation only if asked. Confirm the date, local time, party size, cancellation terms, and any required deposit through the normal approval flow.
