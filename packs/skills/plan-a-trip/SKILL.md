1. Confirm dates, origin, travelers, budget and interests (check memory first).
2. Research flights/trains, neighborhoods to stay in, and 3-5 things per day with opening hours and travel times; cite sources.
3. Build a day-by-day itinerary with times, addresses and backups for bad weather.
4. List what to book and by when, with links. Don't book or pay: that's the user's call.
5. Save the trip to memory (dates, destination) and offer to add events to the calendar.
