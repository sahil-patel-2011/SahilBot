---
name: patient-education
description: Write clear patient education material (handouts, FAQs, after-visit instructions).
source: you
uses: 0
category: Medical
---

1. Confirm the condition or procedure, the audience and the clinic's preferences (check memory and rules).
2. Base content on guidelines and reputable patient resources; cite them for the clinician's review.
3. Write at about a 6th-8th grade reading level: short sentences, plain words, defined terms, headings and bullets.
4. Cover what it is, what to expect, how to prepare and recover, warning signs that need a call or ER visit, and contact placeholders.
5. Mark it "for clinician review before use".
